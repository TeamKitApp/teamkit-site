Privacy first

Built for groups with kids on board

Your trip details stay with your group. Each app is locked, unlisted and kept to what people actually need.

  • Passcode protected. The trip details are encrypted and only open with your group's passcode.
  • No player or guest names. Contacts are listed by role, like Head Coach or Team Manager.
  • Not on search engines. Apps are unlisted and tell search engines not to index them.
  • No accounts or tracking. Nobody signs up, and we don't collect data from the people who use your app.

FAQ

Common questions

Do people need to download anything?

No. It's a web app that opens from a link in any phone's browser. They can add it to their home screen so it looks and opens like an app.

What do you need from me?

Your schedule, hotel and venue details, and any contacts, notes or packing list you want included. A spreadsheet, PDF, email or photos of a printed itinerary all work. Once we've talked, we'll send you a private link to upload it.

How long does it take?

Most apps are ready within a few business days of receiving your details. Tell us your travel date when you reach out, and we'll let you know if your timeline works.

What if the schedule changes?

Send us the change and we'll update the app. The link stays the same, so everyone sees the new version the next time they open it.

Can it match our team colors and logo?

Yes. Every app is branded with your colors, name and logo.

How much does it cost?

Our introductory Season Pass is $499 for one team's full season, with unlimited users and updates included. There are no per-person or per-trip fees.

What counts as a season?

One team's season of play or event schedule, up to six months. If your group has a different setup, like a one-off trip or several teams, tell us and we'll work it out.
