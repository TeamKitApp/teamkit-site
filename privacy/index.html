Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

The short version: we collect only what we need to build your app, we never sell it, and we delete it after your season.

Who we are

TeamKit (teamkitapp.com) is run by Tony Clark. Questions about this policy go to tony@teamkitapp.com.

What we collect

  • Our website form: your name, email, phone (optional), team or group name, type of group, dates, group size, location and any message you add. We use it only to reply to you.
  • Information for your app: the schedule, venues, hotel details, contacts by role, logos and notes you send us to build your app.

We use Google Workspace to store this information and receive email. Our website and apps are hosted by web hosting services (currently GitHub Pages), which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information for marketing.
  • Our website and apps don't use ads, analytics or tracking cookies.
  • People using your app don't create accounts or give us any personal information.

Kids' privacy

Many of our apps are for youth teams, so we keep children's information out of them. We ask clients not to send player names, photos of children, room numbers or home addresses, and we leave out anything like that if it's sent. Contacts are listed by role, such as Head Coach or Team Manager. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

How your app is protected

  • The app's content is encrypted and opens only with your group's passcode.
  • Apps tell search engines not to list them.
  • Items like the packing checklist, and the passcode if someone chooses to remember it, are saved only on that person's phone.
  • Directions buttons open the phone's maps app, which has its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We take your app down and delete the files you sent within 30 days after your season ends, unless you renew. We keep website inquiries for up to one year, then delete them.

Your choices

Email tony@teamkitapp.com any time to see, correct or delete your information, or to take your app down early.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above.
